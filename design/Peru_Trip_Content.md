<a id="weather-september-50-year-averages"></a>
## WEATHER (September, ~50-year averages)

Good window: dry-season conditions, thinner crowds. Plan for the day/night temperature swing more than rain. Strong high-altitude sun burns even when cool — sunscreen daily.

| Stop | Elevation | Day high | Night low | Rain | The real story |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lima | Sea level | ~20°C | ~15°C | Near-zero | Grey, damp coastal fog (garúa); little sun |
| Cusco | 3,400 m | ~21°C | ~4°C | Mostly dry | Warm sun, extreme UV, cold nights |
| Sacred Valley / Machu Picchu | 2,000–2,900 m | ~17°C | ~6°C | Odd shower | Milder days, cloud-forest humidity |
| Puno / Lake Titicaca | 3,800 m | ~16°C | ~0 to −1°C | Very dry | Intense sun by day, freezing nights |

## DAY-BY-DAY

<a id="wed-sept-16-arrival-lady-bee"></a>
### Wed, Sept 16 — Arrival + Lady Bee
- Overnight: Barranco or Miraflores, Lima
- Weather: ~20°/15°. Grey & mild; coastal fog, high humidity, little sun. Light jacket, not a raincoat.
- At a glance: Land off the redeye at 6:55 AM — take it easy. Check in, nap, gentle wander, then 8 PM cocktails at Lady Bee.
- Logistics: Land 6:55 AM · Airport→Miraflores 30–90 min · Taxi: Green Taxi ~PEN 100.
- ★ Reservation 8:00 PM — Lady Bee. Wed Sept 16, 20:00. Av. Pedro de Osma 205, Barranco. Phone +51 1 438 3775. Peru's top-ranked cocktail bar (World's 50 Best); small plates too — arrive a little hungry.
- Ease into it:
  - Malecón de Miraflores (Miraflores) — clifftop boardwalk over the Pacific; paragliders, ocean air, sunset. Walk to Parque del Amor.
  - Parque Kennedy / Cat Park (Miraflores) — dozens of friendly resident cats, artists, food carts. Grab picarones from a cart.
  - Ceviche lunch — get the classic in today so Maido stands alone tomorrow. See recommendations for La Mar, Canta Rana, Isolina.

<a id="thu-sept-17-lima-culture-maido"></a>
### Thu, Sept 17 — Lima culture + Maido
- Overnight: Barranco or Miraflores, Lima
- Weather: ~20°/15°. Same soft, overcast day; comfortable for walking and museums; light layer for the evening.
- At a glance: A full Lima day now you've recovered. Keep lunch light — tonight is Maido. Culture-heavy by day so you arrive hungry.
- ★ Reservation, evening — Maido. Calle San Martín 399, Miraflores. Nikkei tasting menu; reconfirm day before, flag dietary needs, smart-casual, arrive hungry.
- Do this by day:
  - ★ Larco Museum (Pueblo Libre) — world-class pre-Columbian art in a garden mansion; the single best museum in Lima. Beautiful café on site. Address: Av. Simón Bolívar 1515, Pueblo Libre, Lima.
  - Convento de San Francisco (Historic Centre) — Baroque monastery with underground catacomb ossuary and a 20,000-volume antique library. Guided entry only.
  - ★ Barranco street-art walk — Bajada de Baños, Pasaje Oroya, and the Bridge of Sighs. Lima's open-air mural gallery. Golden hour is best.
  - MALI, Museo Amano, MAC Lima — optional extra museums if the day runs long. See recommendations.

<a id="fri-sept-18-tour-day-1-lima-tour-begins"></a>
### Fri, Sept 18 — Tour Day 1: Lima (tour begins)
- Overnight: Hotel, Lima (Miraflores)
- Weather: ~20°/15°. Mild & grey; easy walking weather.
- At a glance: Tour starts with a 2 PM welcome meeting and an easy leader-led walk downtown. You've already seen Lima well — today is about meeting the group.
- Logistics: Welcome meeting 2:00 PM · at Hotel El Señorial · bring insurance + emergency contacts.
- Journey: Miraflores → (leader-led walk) → Downtown Lima.
- If you have the afternoon:
  - Huaca Pucllana (Miraflores) — pre-Inca adobe pyramid rising out of the city; lit at night, restaurant on site.
  - Optional group dinner — relaxed first night with fellow travellers; leader will suggest a spot.
- Note: don't miss the 2 PM meeting — it's where insurance & emergency details are collected. Late? Tell reception.

<a id="sat-sept-19-tour-day-2-fly-to-cusco"></a>
### Sat, Sept 19 — Tour Day 2: Fly to Cusco
- Overnight: Hotel, Cusco (3,450 m)
- Weather: ~21°/4°. The big swing begins — warm sunny afternoons, ~4°C nights. Extreme UV: hat + sunscreen by day, warm layer at night.
- At a glance: Early flight to Cusco (5:30 AM hotel departure). Main job is acclimatising — go slow, coca tea, skip alcohol. Gentle orientation walk introduces the old town.
- Logistics: Depart hotel 5:30 AM · flight ~1h 20m · altitude 3,450 m · take it SLOW.
- Journey: Lima → (early flight) → Cusco.
- Orientation-walk highlights:
  - Qorikancha (Av. El Sol) — Inca Sun Temple with a Spanish church on top; finest stonework in Cusco.
  - Plaza de Armas & San Blas (Old Town) — grand main square, then the steep artisan quarter with the best city views.
  - San Pedro Market (Central Cusco) — juices, cheeses, chocolate, caldo de gallina; the essential local market.

<a id="sun-sept-20-tour-day-3-sacred-valley-aguas-calientes"></a>
### Sun, Sept 20 — Tour Day 3: Sacred Valley → Aguas Calientes
- Overnight: Aguas Calientes (cloud forest)
- Weather: ~18°/6°. Milder valley day; dropping into cloud forest, humidity rises — keep a rain layer handy.
- At a glance: Scenic run through the Sacred Valley with a community visit & lunch, then the train from Ollantaytambo to Aguas Calientes at the foot of Machu Picchu.
- Logistics: Travel ~3 hrs + train · included community lunch · sit on the left side of the train.
- Journey: Cusco → Sacred Valley → (train) → Aguas Calientes.
- Along the way / that evening:
  - Maras Salt Mines (Sacred Valley) — thousands of terraced salt pools worked since pre-Inca times; very photogenic. Buy salt & salt-chocolate. Address: Salineras de Maras.
  - Ollantaytambo (Sacred Valley) — living Inca town with a monumental fortress-temple and still-flowing Inca waterworks.
  - ★ Indio Feliz (Aguas Calientes) — the town's most-loved restaurant; quirky French-Peruvian; perfect post-arrival dinner.
- Note: today is the annual Warachicuy Inca reenactment at Sacsayhuamán in Cusco — you'll be in the valley, so you'll miss it, but locals may mention it.

<a id="mon-sept-21-tour-day-4-machu-picchu-back-to-cusco"></a>
### Mon, Sept 21 — Tour Day 4: Machu Picchu ★, back to Cusco
- Overnight: Hotel, Cusco
- Weather: ~15°/5°. Cloud forest; highest shower chance of the trip; misty mornings often clear. Layers + rain shell.
- At a glance: Early bus up the switchbacks for a guided tour of one of the Seven Wonders of the World, then train & drive back to Cusco.
- Logistics: First bus 6:00 AM · bring passport (checked) · also water, bug spray, rain layer.
- Journey: Aguas Calientes → (bus up) → Machu Picchu → Cusco.
- Notes: site opens after sunrise (no sunrise visit); once the guided tour ends you must exit. Wayna Picchu needs a separate permit booked months ahead — Intrepid leaders can't help arrange it, so sort it yourself early if you want the climb.
- Back in Cusco tonight:
  - Cicciolina (Centre, 2nd floor, Calle Triunfo 393) — long-loved creative Andean-Mediterranean cooking; a great celebratory dinner.
  - Museo del Pisco (Central Cusco) — pisco bar with tastings & live music; a fun, low-key night.

<a id="tue-sept-22-tour-day-5-free-day-in-cusco"></a>
### Tue, Sept 22 — Tour Day 5: Free day in Cusco
- Overnight: Hotel, Cusco
- Weather: ~21°/4°. Classic Cusco; bright sunny ~21°C, cold once the sun dips. Great for a high hike — start early, layer up.
- At a glance: Your one truly flexible day. Big-adventure option (Rainbow Mountain, Humantay) or a relaxed art-market-and-café crawl through San Blas — a lighter day here is smart before Titicaca.
- Active options:
  - Palcoyo Rainbow Mountain (day trip, ~USD 100) — quieter, easier alternative to Vinicunca; three rainbow ridges, less crowd. Long day at altitude.
  - Humantay Lake (day trip, ~USD 130) — turquoise glacial lake below a snow peak; gorgeous, a real leg-burner.
- Relaxed option — San Blas:
  - ★ San Blas artisan quarter — cobbled galleries & workshops; Casa Mérida ceramics, Mendívil figures, the 7 Angelitos alley. Address: Plazoleta San Blas, Cusco.
  - ChocoMuseo (Plaza Regocijo 216) — bean-to-bar chocolate workshops and a pisco/coffee/chocolate tasting; fun and hands-on.
- Wildlife + art half-day:
  - ★ Cochahuasi Animal Sanctuary (Km 22 Cusco–Pisac road) — condor, bear and puma rescue on the Pisac road; ticket funds the release program.
  - Awana Kancha (Cusco–Pisac road) — llamas, alpacas, vicuñas and guanacos to feed, plus live weaving. Pairs with Cochahuasi.
  - Museo de Arte Precolombino (MAP) (Plazoleta Nazarenas 231) — if you'd rather stay in town.

<a id="wed-sept-23-tour-day-6-drive-to-puno"></a>
### Wed, Sept 23 — Tour Day 6: Drive to Puno
- Overnight: Hotel, Puno (3,800 m)
- Weather: ~16°/−1°. Higher & colder; sunny dry days, nights near/below freezing. Dry air makes the sun feel fierce and the cold bite hard.
- At a glance: Long, scenic drive (~7.5 hrs) across the high Altiplano to Puno on Lake Titicaca. The landscape — endless plains, alpaca herds — is the attraction.
- Logistics: Drive ~7.5 hrs · altitude 3,800 m · try tonight: grilled trout (trucha).
- Journey: Cusco → (Altiplano drive) → Puno.
- In Puno:
  - Jirón Lima (Puno centre) — the pedestrian spine; alpaca-wool shops, cafés, trout restaurants; best stroll in town.
  - Kuntur Wasi / Condor Hill (Puno) — steep climb to a giant condor statue and the best view over the lake; sunset if you have energy.
- Note: Intrepid flags occasional political unrest around Puno — the leader will adjust transport if needed.

<a id="thu-sept-24-tour-day-7-lake-titicaca-and-homestay"></a>
### Thu, Sept 24 — Tour Day 7: Lake Titicaca & homestay ★
- Overnight: Homestay, Lake Titicaca
- Weather: ~16°/−1°. Your coldest night — ~0 to −1°C, no heating (blankets provided). Bright & sunny on the water; thermals + fleece after dark.
- At a glance: Motorboat across the world's highest navigable lake to the Uros floating reed islands, then a homestay with a local family — consistently a trip highlight.
- Logistics: Uros islands by motorboat · tonight: family homestay · facilities basic, drop toilets, no showers.
- Make the most of it:
  - ★ Uros Floating Islands (Lake Titicaca) — islands hand-built from totora reeds; one of the most surreal places on the trip.
  - Your homestay (Lake Titicaca) — lean in: help cook, try Quechua, join the soccer game. Bring small gifts (fruit from Puno market, school supplies).

<a id="fri-sept-25-tour-day-8-fly-back-to-lima"></a>
### Fri, Sept 25 — Tour Day 8: Fly back to Lima
- Overnight: Hotel, Lima (free evening)
- Weather: ~20°/15°. Back to mild & grey in Lima; perfect for a last easy evening in Miraflores or Barranco.
- At a glance: Bus to Juliaca, flight to Lima, free final evening. A great chance for one more special meal — keep it in Barranco.
- Logistics: Travel ~half day · evening free.
- Journey: Titicaca → (Juliaca flight) → Lima.
- Farewell-dinner ideas (Barranco):
  - ★ Isolina (Barranco) — José del Castillo's nostalgic Limeño tavern; huge sharing plates; on Latin America's 50 Best. Reserve. Address: Av. San Martín 101, Barranco, Lima.
  - Mayo (at Central's Casa Túpac, Av. Pedro de Osma 301, Barranco) — Central's cocktail bar; a stylish nightcap.
  - La Candelaria (Barranco) — or make it a night out: Afro-Peruvian/criollo peña with live music and dance. Verify show nights.

<a id="sat-sept-26-tour-day-9-depart-lima"></a>
### Sat, Sept 26 — Tour Day 9: Depart Lima

- At a glance: Flight home departs 8:25 AM — really a travel morning. Arrange an airport transfer the night before.

### Lima — Ceviche & seafood (ceviche is a lunch dish; go midday)
- ★ La Mar (Miraflores) — Gastón Acurio's flagship cebichería. Lunch only, no reservations — get the ceviche tasting. Address: Av. Mariscal La Mar 770, Miraflores, Lima.

<a id="lima-street-food-and-sandwiches"></a>
### Lima — Street food & sandwiches
- ★ Grimanesa Vargas Anticuchos (Miraflores) — Peru's most famous anticuchera; beef-heart skewers in panca-chile marinade. Evenings, cash, go early. Address: Calle Ignacio Merino 466, Miraflores, Lima.

- Picarones Mary (Miraflores) — syrup-drenched squash/sweet-potato doughnuts from a Parque Kennedy cart. A must-try.

<a id="lima-special-meals-beyond-maido-and-central"></a>

<a id="lima-art-and-street-art-barranco-is-an-open-air-gallery"></a>
### Lima — Art & street art (Barranco is an open-air gallery)
- ★ Bajada de Baños & Pasaje Oroya (Barranco) — mural epicentre; look for Jade Rivera & Yandy Graffer works near the Bridge of Sighs.
- ★ Dédalo (Barranco) — 30-year institution in a restored mansion: 700+ artists across themed rooms, courtyard café. Address: Jirón Sáenz Peña 295, Barranco, Lima.
- MATE – Museo Mario Testino (Barranco) — the Peruvian photographer's museum; sharp, stylish, compact.

- MALI – Museo de Arte de Lima (Parque de la Exposición) — the city's main fine-art museum, pre-Columbian through contemporary.
- Museo Amano (Miraflores) — exquisite Chancay textiles; small, timed entry.
- MAC Lima (Barranco) — contemporary art; pairs with the Barranco street-art walk.

<a id="lima-history-and-oddities"></a>
### Lima — History & oddities
- ★ Museo Larco (Pueblo Libre) — one of the world's great museums; pre-Columbian gold, ceramics, the famous erotic gallery. Address: Av. Simón Bolívar 1515, Pueblo Libre, Lima.
- San Francisco Catacombs (Historic Centre) — underground ossuary beneath a Baroque monastery; used as a cemetery until 1810. Guided only.
- Casa Aliaga (Historic Centre) — viceregal mansion gifted by Pizarro, still owned by the same family 480+ years on. By tour. Address: Jirón de la Unión 224, Lima.
- Magic Water Circuit (Parque de la Reserva) — choreographed illuminated fountains at night; kitschy, huge, genuinely fun.

<a id="animals-and-wildlife"></a>
### Animals & wildlife

- ★ Cochahuasi Animal Sanctuary (Sacred Valley, Km 22 Cusco–Pisac road, ~30 min from Cusco) — family-run rescue for Andean condors, spectacled (Andean) bears, pumas, macaws and vicuñas; ticket funds the release program. Ethical and right on the way to Pisac.
- Awana Kancha (Cusco–Pisac road) — "living museum" of llamas, alpacas, vicuñas and guanacos you can feed, plus live weaving demos. Quick stop; pairs with Cochahuasi.
- Islas Palomino (Lima, boats from Callao) — boat tour to swim with a wild sea-lion colony; also seabirds and penguins. Half-day.
- Pantanos de Villa (Chorrillos, Lima) — protected coastal wetland with ~200 bird species including flamingos. Easy half-day.
- Inkaterra Machu Picchu Pueblo (Aguas Calientes) — spectacled-bear rescue project and cloud-forest birding. Verify access for non-guests.
- Mandor Gardens (Aguas Calientes) — birding and waterfall walk from town.

<a id="cusco-eat-and-drink-picanterías-for-the-real-thing-san-blas-for-cafés"></a>
### Cusco — Eat & drink (picanterías for the real thing; San Blas for cafés)
- ★ Pachapapa (San Blas) — open-air courtyard with a clay oven on Plazoleta San Blas; roast dishes, cuy for the brave.
- La Cusqueñísima (Magisterio) — family picantería since 1986, ~20 min from centre; baked cuy, choclo con queso, sarzas.
- Cicciolina (Centre) — creative Andean-Mediterranean upstairs on Calle Triunfo; Cusco's enduring favourite. Reserve. Address: Calle Triunfo 393, Cusco.
- Green Point (San Blas) — Cusco's best-known vegan spot; even for omnivores, a great-value tasting lunch.

<a id="cusco-art-history-and-skate"></a>
### Cusco — Art, history & skate
- ★ San Blas artisan quarter — Casa Mérida ceramics, Mendívil figures, the 7 Angelitos alley; the artist heart of Cusco. Address: Plazoleta San Blas, Cusco.
- Twelve-Angled Stone (Hatun Rumiyoc) — famous perfectly-fitted Inca stone in an old palace wall; a two-minute marvel.
- Qorikancha (Av. El Sol) — the Inca Temple of the Sun beneath Santo Domingo church; small site museum attached.
- Skatepark La Florida (Barrio La Florida) — basic free concrete park; the spot to meet local skaters/fingerboarders. Community is social-media-run; ask around.
- ★ Museo de Arte Precolombino / MAP (Cusco) — Larco's sister collection (gold, silver, ceramics) in a gorgeous mansion, with MAP Café — one of Cusco's best restaurants — in the courtyard. Address: Plazoleta Nazarenas 231, Cusco.
- Museo Machupicchu / Casa Concha (Cusco) — the artifacts Bingham took to Yale, now returned; the context you don't get at the site itself.

<a id="sacred-valley"></a>
### Sacred Valley
- ★ Mil (near Moray) — Virgilio Martínez's high-altitude tasting menu beside the Moray terraces. A destination in itself — book far ahead.
- Chuncho (Ollantaytambo) — Andean techniques on Andean ingredients; roast lamb, cuy, chicha, simply plated.
- Moray (Sacred Valley) — concentric circular Inca terraces; an agricultural 'laboratory' of microclimates.
- Pisac Market & Ruins (Pisac) — hilltop ruins with a solar observatory, plus one of the valley's best craft markets.

<a id="aguas-calientes-machu-picchu-base-town-food-costs-more-it-all-arrives-by-train"></a>
### Aguas Calientes (Machu Picchu base town; food costs more — it all arrives by train)
- ★ Indio Feliz — the town's most-loved spot; colourful French-Peruvian; a rewarding post-hike dinner.
- Café de París (Av. Pachacutec) — French-owned patisserie; best bread/pastries in town and box lunches for the mountain.
- Town Food Market (main plaza) — cheapest eats in town; set menus from ~8 soles. Try fresh Andean trout.

<a id="puno-and-lake-titicaca"></a>
### Puno & Lake Titicaca
- ★ Taquile Island (Lake Titicaca) — UNESCO-listed for textiles woven by the men; peaceful, car-free, fresh-trout lunch.
- Sillustani (Lake Umayo, ~35 km) — pre-Inca Qolla funerary towers (chullpas) on a peninsula; magic at golden hour.
- Puno Cathedral (Puno centre) — Andean-Baroque façade on the main plaza; a quick, striking stop.
- Yavarí Steamship (Puno lakefront) — a 160-year-old British-built iron steamship, now a floating museum. Delightfully odd.
